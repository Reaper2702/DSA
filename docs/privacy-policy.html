Privacy Policy — Trump Cards

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Trump Cards does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app has no network access, no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising.

What the app collects

Nothing. Trump Cards is a single-player card game that runs entirely on your device. It does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information, and it has no way to send information anywhere.

Permissions

The released app requests no Android permissions at all. It cannot access the internet, storage, the camera, the microphone, or your location.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

There are none. The app contains no analytics SDK, no advertising network, no crash reporting service, and no tracking identifiers. No third-party code in the app transmits anything about you or your device.

Data stored on your device

Game state — the cards in play and the score of the current match — exists only while the app is running and is discarded when you close it. Nothing is written to a server.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either.

The cards

The cricketers in the deck are fictional. Their names and statistics were invented for the game and do not describe any real person.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app collects anything, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
